Our events · bringing Matara together

Community events at Sakura Preschool

Beyond the classroom, Sakura is proud to stand with our community.

Concert · September 2026

වරෙන් හීන් සැරේ · මාතරටWaren Heen Sare Matarata

A musical evening honouring Gurudevi Sunil Santha, the Father of Modern Sinhala Music, with his timeless songs brought to life by a new generation of singers.

  • Saturday, 19th September 2026 · 6.30 PM onwards
  • Sanasa Hall, Nupe, Matara
  • Sakura Pre School was an official ticket outlet

Thank you to the Leo Club of Rahula College, Iyon Lanka, the artists, and every family who joined us for a beautiful night.

Poson Dansal · May–June 2026

මහා පහන් කූඩු රාජයා සමඟින් මහා දන්සැල් පින්කමGrand Lantern & Dansal Pinkama

A giant pahan kudu (Poson lantern) lit up the Sakura Preschool grounds while we served free ice cream and sago to everyone who came by.

  • Sunday, 31st May 2026 · 4.00 PM onwards · Ice cream dansal
  • Tuesday, 2nd June 2026 · 5.00 PM onwards · Sago dansal
  • Sakura Preschool grounds, Matara

Thank you to every family and neighbour who joined us to share in this pinkama.

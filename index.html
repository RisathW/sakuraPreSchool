Little roots, big growth

A play-based preschool in Matara since 2000

Sakura is named for the blossom, because every child, like every flower, opens up in their own time. Sakura Pre School has been welcoming Matara’s little ones since our first day on 8th January 2000.

Our motto: “They Grow We Clone.”

Play is how they learn

Blocks, art, music, and pretend play build focus, motor skills, and confidence, long before a desk and worksheet ever could.

Sinhala & English, every day

Daily songs, stories, and conversation in both languages, so children arrive at Grade 1 already comfortable with letters, sounds, and simple words.

Ready, gently, for school

Routines, sharing, and following along in a group, the everyday skills that make the first day of “real” school feel familiar, not frightening.

Good to know before you visit

Preschool admissions: frequently asked questions

What ages do you accept?
Children aged 2½ to 5 (Grade-1 ready), across our Play Group (2½–3 years), LKG (3–4 years) and UKG (4–5 years). Children move up when they’re developmentally ready, not strictly by birthday.

What languages does my child learn?
Sinhala and English, through daily songs, stories, and conversation, the two languages every child needs for primary school. No prior experience is needed for your child or for you.

What curriculum do you follow?
Activity-based, play-first learning designed to build creativity, critical thinking, and confidence, in a safe, child-friendly environment.

Do you offer a sibling discount or fee assistance?
Ask our director about sibling rates and fee assistance when you visit. We’re happy to talk through what works for your family.

What should my child bring on the first day?
A labeled bag, a water bottle, and a full change of clothes. We provide art smocks and everything else.

Can we visit before enrolling?
Absolutely, visits run weekday mornings, 9:00–11:00 AM, so you can watch a real morning circle. Use the form below, call, or message us to pick a time.
